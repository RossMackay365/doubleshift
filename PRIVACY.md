# Privacy Policy

**DoubleShift: Training Scheduler**
Last updated: 13 November 2026

DoubleShift doesn't collect, send or share any of your information.

- Your schedules are saved as files in the DoubleShift folder in your Documents. They stay on your computer.
- The app has no accounts, analytics, tracking or ads.
- The app only goes online when you choose a link, which opens in your web browser.

If you install DoubleShift from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft's [privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement) covers the download and updates.

Questions? Please [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/RossMackay365/doubleshift/issues).
